AlphaFold2 Structure Prediction in Google Colab

This lab will teach you how to use the AlphaFold2 protein structure prediction tool through a Google Colab notebook. You will learn how to input a protein sequence, run the AlphaFold2 pipeline, and interpret the predicted 3D structure output.

Programming Skill: None
Primary Course: Foundational Module
Also for: Biochemistry, Research Methods, Computational Biology, Structural Biology, Molecular Biology
Format: Multi-Part Module
Authors: Dr. Lori D. Banks
Estimated Time: 1-2 hours
Piloted with: Undergraduate - Third or Fourth Year
Students Tested: 32

Overview

This activity provides students with hands-on experience using the AlphaFold2 tool to predict protein structures, bridging their understanding of protein structure and function with their growing knowledge of cyberinfrastructure skills. This is a low-entry activity that includes aspects of a GUI (i.e., buttons and pull-down menus) environment with Python to introduce the students to the code. The major objective is to get students to be familiar enough with the notebook interface to run premade code, and possibly to modify it to meet their specific computational needs. |

Instructor Notes

Additional context for instructors using this lesson:

• This lesson was designed to introduce students to the relationship between protein structure and function through computational analysis. It assumes that students have had previous instruction in the basics of the biological functions of the molecules that they are observing.
• Prerequisites for the course include General Inorganic Chemistry and General Biology for Biology majors
• Consider assigning another protein (primary amino acid sequence) as homework rather than in-class work

Timing notes:
• The module was designed for a 110-minute class session, but may require additional time for students to complete the exercises and explore the output using other visualization programs.

Common student questions:
• "How does changing the prediction settings change the output?"
• "What is the meaning/interpretation/units of the pLDDT score?"
• "How can I interpret the results if the confidence is low? What can I do next to improve the prediction?"
